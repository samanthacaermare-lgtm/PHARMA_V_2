About PHARMA-SEE

Mission: To make access to medicines easier, faster, and safer for every member of the community.

The problem

Long waits, not knowing if a medicine is in stock, and trouble tracking refills.

The solution

See live stock and prices, reserve online, and pick up at a set time.

Who we help

Seniors

Large text and simple steps.

Caregivers

Reserve and track for someone else.

Parents

Quick pickups with less waiting.

Maintenance patients

Refill reminders you can count on.

Pharmacy staff

Clear inventory and order tools.

Contact

Emergency? Call your local hospital emergency services right away.
